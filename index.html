About

The DJ Who Lives for Your Big Day

Hi, I'm DJ Klick — a Truckee, CA-based wedding DJ with over 10 years of experience turning receptions into legendary nights. As an open-format DJ, I seamlessly blend multiple genres to keep every generation on the dance floor. I serve the greater Tahoe/Reno area and beyond.

My philosophy? Simple. Fun. Professional.

Simple — No complicated packages or hidden fees. Just great music and seamless execution.
Fun — I read the room, build energy, and keep the dance floor packed all night.
Professional — Reliable, organized, and dedicated to making your day flawless.

Every couple is unique, and every playlist is tailored to your vision. Whether you want romantic ballads during dinner or a packed-dance-floor finale, I've got you covered. I work closely with you before the big day to make sure every moment hits exactly right.

Services
